## Recording checklist

1. Use a clean browser profile at 1440 by 900 or 1920 by 1080.
2. Hide bookmarks, notifications, email addresses, and browser extensions.
3. Use one clearly labeled QA trade or a genuine historical trade you are comfortable showing.
4. Never display API keys, database credentials, or a private inbox.
5. Keep cursor movement slow and pause after every navigation.
6. Remove the QA trade and rule after recording.
7. Place the disclosure "AI-generated voice" in the video description or end card.
